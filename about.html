About Isaac

Hi, I'm Isaac.

I'm a vintage treasure hunter based just outside of Philadelphia. I live for the thrill of uncovering Victorian brooches, Art Deco rings, and sterling silver pieces that have stood the test of time.

My mission is simple: "Making timeless treasure affordable." Whether it's 9.925 sterling, gold-filled, or solid gold—every piece has a story, and I want to pass that story on to you.

Come hang out, bid, and score some deals. Let's find the good stuff together. ✨
